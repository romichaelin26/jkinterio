Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials do you use for modular kitchens?

We use 100% BWP 710 Grade Marine Plywood for cabinet carcasses to prevent water expansion. Surface finishes include Anti-Scratch Acrylic, PU Polish, Natural Veneer, and High-Gloss Laminate with soft-close Hettich or Blum hardware.

Do you serve all locations across Kerala?

Yes, we provide site inspection, 3D design consultation, and turnkey installation across all 14 districts in Kerala, including Kollam, Trivandrum, Kochi, Kottayam, Alappuzha, and Kozhikode.

Can you custom fabricate steel furniture and staircases?

Absolutely! Building on the legacy of JK Steel & Wood Industries, we operate an in-house steel fabrication unit for SS 304 staircases, balcony glass railings, laser-cut gates, and industrial steel workstations.

How long does a full home interior project take?

A standard 3BHK interior project takes approximately 35 to 45 days from 3D design approval to final site handover.
